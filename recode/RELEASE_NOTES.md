## 🔧 Blipville Recode (v0.5.0, early beta)

The desktop app, rebuilt from scratch on a new engine (Qt).

- ✅ **No more black flashes**: real see-through windows
- ✅ **Proper right-click menus**
- ✅ **Your save carries over**: same pets, hearts and unlocks as the classic app
- ✅ All 249 Blips and their colors, hats, petting, feeding, naps, tossing, name tags
- ✅ Updates itself, like the classic app

**Still coming to the Recode:** Mystery Eggs, the Collection, cryptids/kaiju/legends, Worlds and weather, the hat shop, Night Shift and sounds. Until then, the classic Blipville has them.

**Try it:** download `Blipville-Recode.exe` below. Quit the classic Blipville first, so they don't both move your pets around.

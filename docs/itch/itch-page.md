## Description

Paste everything between the lines into the **Description** box:

---

**Blipville** is a little town of pixel pets that live on top of your taskbar. 🏘️

They wander around, nap, chase your mouse, eat cookies, and say hi to each other by name. You can even pick them up and throw them. (They're fine. Mostly.)

**🐾 55 kinds of pets, 154 colors**
Cats, dogs, foxes, frogs, axolotls, capybaras, dragons, unicorns, sharks and many more, each with its own real-life colors, personality and way of moving. Frogs hop, bats flutter, whales float, and sloths are *very* slow.

**What you can do**
- **Click** a pet to pet it 💕
- **Drag and throw** it. It bounces off the edges of your screen
- **Feed it cookies** 🍪 when it gets hungry
- **Add friends**: up to 12 pets at once, and they greet each other
- **Rename** them, change their kind and color, show name tags
- Let one **follow your mouse** around

**🧪 This is a beta.** New animals are on the way. Want one added? Suggest it on GitHub or in the comments below!

**Windows may say "Windows protected your PC"** the first time, because the app isn't signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**. Blipville is open source, so anyone can read every line of code: https://github.com/Johnblip189/blipville

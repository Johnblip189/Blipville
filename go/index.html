How to play
  • Tap a Blip to pet it 💖
  • Drag a Blip to pick it up. Let go to toss it!
  • Press and hold a Blip for its menu: feed, nap, rename, colors.
  • Caring earns hearts: pet +1 · feed +3 (+5 if hungry) · full nap +2 · daily treat +10
  • Spend 30 hearts on a 🥚 Mystery Egg to hatch one of the rare Blips.
  • 👣 Keep watching: every few minutes a cryptid sneaks through town. Tap the shadow with glowing eyes before it gets away to befriend it!
  • 🌍 Tap Worlds to unlock new places (Plant, Sweets, Ocean, Dino, Space) with hearts and pick their Blips.
  • 🦖 Once in a long while a giant kaiju stomps through. Tap it 3 times before it leaves!
  • 👻 At night (7 PM to 7 AM) ghosts drift by. They fade in and out: tap one while it glows! A 🕯️ Spirit Candle (in the 📖 Zoo) calls them in the daytime.
  • 👁️ Watch out for the Lurker, a shadow ghost with lots of eyes. It's the only Blip that can ✨ Awaken 3 times, all the way to Lurker Eternal!
  • Some Blips can ✨ Awaken into a new form with an Awakening Crystal (in their menu).
  • 🏆 Tap the trophy at the top to see your trophies. Each one earns bonus hearts.
  • Brave enough? Tap 🌙 Night to play Night Shift. Survive until 6 AM to unlock the robo crew!
  • Buy hats & outfits from a Blip's menu. Tap 🔇 at the top to turn on sound effects.
  • On a laptop: right-click a Blip for its menu, Space pets the Blip under your mouse, and F feed · N friend · E egg · Z zoo · S share · Esc close. Press 📌 Pop out to keep your Blips in a little window on top of everything while you work!
  • 💬 Join the Blipville Discord to share your town, get help and suggest Blips!
  • 💻 Get it as an app: on a Windows laptop or Chromebook, press ⬇ Install app at the top (or the install icon at the right end of the address bar in Edge or Chrome). Blipville gets its own icon in the Start menu and its own window, and plays offline. On a phone: Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android).
💾 Save code
Your town saves on this device automatically. To back it up or move it to another phone, browser or the Windows app (right-click a pet → 💾 Save code), copy your save code, then paste it there and tap Load.

## ✨ Blipville Redux (v0.5, early beta)

The desktop app, remade with a brand-new way of drawing your pets.

- ✅ **No more black flashes**: every pet window is a real see-through picture now, so clicking a pet, opening a menu or
  dragging it around doesn't make Windows repaint anything
- ✅ **Your save carries over**: same pets, hearts and unlocks as the classic app
- ✅ Everything from the classic app: all the Blips and colors, hats, Mystery Eggs, the Collection, cryptids, kaiju and
  World Legends, Worlds, weather, games, sounds, Night Shift and the Beta Buddy gift
- ✅ Updates itself, like the classic app

**Try it:** download `Blipville-Redux.exe` below. Quit the classic Blipville first, so they don't both move your pets around.

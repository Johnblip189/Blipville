# 🧸 Blipville figures: Series 1

Posable pixel figures built straight from the game sprites (`python3 make_figures.py` rebuilds them all).

| Figure | Rarity | File |
|---|---|---|
| Silvertear-03 | ⭐⭐⭐ ultra-rare | `silvertear03_figure.stl` |
| Blip | ⭐⭐ rare | `blip_figure.stl` |
| T-Rex | regular | `trex_figure.stl` |
| Wendigo | regular | `wendigo_figure.stl` |
| Bun | regular | `bun_figure.stl` |
| Beta Buddy | regular | `beta_buddy_figure.stl` |

Extras (not in the series yet): `robo_bear_figure.stl`, `boo_figure.stl`, `spike_figure.stl`.

**Printing:** each STL is one plate: the figure on its back, two arms and two legs (a left and a right: the toes point
the other way). No supports, PLA, 0.2 mm, 3 walls.

**Putting it together:** press the arms into the shoulder holes and the legs into the hip notches (from the side, toes
forward). Arms turn all the way round; legs swing about 80 degrees forward and 90 back (walk, sit, kick). Everything
holds its pose by friction. Loose joint: a turn of tape on the peg. Tight joint: sand the peg a little (or lower
`PEG_D` and rebuild).

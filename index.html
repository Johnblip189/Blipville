💜 Blipville 1.0

Thank you to everyone who played the betas. Every pet you hatched, cryptid you spotted and idea you sent helped make this.

The Redux engine

Blipville used to hide a "magic pink" background, and Windows had to repaint it whenever you clicked a pet or opened a menu. Sometimes that showed black for a split second. 1.0 draws every pet as a real see-through picture and hands it straight to Windows, so nothing gets repainted and nothing flashes. Text and bubbles got smooth edges too.

Your town comes with you

Same save as before: same pets, names, hats, hearts, worlds and unlocks. Already playing? Blipville (and Blipville Redux) offer the 1.0 update by themselves. Just say yes.

72 new special Blips

12 more in every tier (24 more Cryptids!): Rare egg Blips like the Qilin and Rainbow Blip, Cryptids like the Ningen, Lusca and Beast of Gévaudan, Kaiju like Clockwork Rex, Ghosts like the Haunted Teacup, and World Legends like the Star Whale.

13 new Blips

Say hi to the Red Panda, Polar Bear, Puffin, Beluga, Gorilla, Sugar Glider, Highland Cow, Shoebill, Tarsier, Marmot, Mantis Shrimp, Frilled Lizard and the Echidna. That's 380 kinds of pets!

Friendship & Cookie Catch

Every Blip now bonds with you as you care for it, all the way from New friend to Soulmate, with hearts at every level. And in the new minigame Cookie Catch, your Blip catches falling cookies (watch out for burnt ones!).

Only on Windows: they climb your windows

Blips jump up onto your open windows, walk along the top and nap there. Drag the window and they ride along; close it and they tumble back down. Wiggle your mouse near one and it chases and pounces on the pointer, and sometimes it steals it for a second!

The Arena

Pick a team of 3 and battle, turn by turn: choose from 4 moves each turn! Copy your battle code and send it to a friend so they can battle your team, or take on five practice rivals. 10 types, from 🔥 Spark and ⚡ Volt to 🍬 Sweet and 🪐 Cosmic: each element is strong against 2 others, and some Blips have 2 types.

Awakened forms

20 Blips can awaken into powered-up forms, like the Nine-Tail Kitsune, Inferno Magmaw, King Sheetie and Lunar Mothman. Every kaiju has one! In the Arena you get one Awaken per battle. And the new ghost, the many-eyed Lurker, awakens three times, all the way up to Lurker Eternal, and then the whole battle moves to a giant dam held up by a wall of Lurkers. 👁️

Ghosts

A brand-new tier: 25 ghosts drift past haunted houses and graveyards at night. They fade in and out, so tap one while it glows! Light a 🕯️ Spirit Candle to call them in the daytime.

Trophies

33 trophies to earn, from petting your first Blip to befriending every ghost. Each one gives bonus hearts.

Keeps itself up to date

When a new version is out, Blipville asks, downloads it and restarts by itself. Right-click a pet → 🔄 Updates to check any time.

Getting it

  1. Download Blipville.exe and double-click it. No installer needed.
  2. Windows may say "protected your PC" the first time. Click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Your pets drop in from the top of the screen. Right-click one for everything else. 🎉

Found a bug? Right-click a pet → 💬 Suggestions & help → Get help / report a problem (and Copy error log helps a lot).

Play anywhere

Laptop & Chromebook

Play right in Edge or Chrome, or install it as an app: it gets its own icon in the Start menu, its own window, and works offline. Perfect for school and work laptops that don't allow downloads. Use 📌 Pop out to keep your Blips on top while you work.

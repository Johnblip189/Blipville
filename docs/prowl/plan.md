# Prowl Week (event plan, draft)

Follows Spiky Week (ends December 3), same rules and pause days.

- Pets that count: Lion, Tiger, Cheetah, Jaguar. Pet and feed them, plus daily quests, to earn event points.
- 4 event skins at **5 points each** (5, 10, 15, 20): Lion **Sunmane**, Tiger **Moonstripe**, Cheetah **Dawnspot**, Jaguar **Emberrose**.
- 7 new Blips at **10 points each** (30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90): **Lioness**, **Cougar**, **Panther**, then **Serval**, **Caracal**, **Ocelot**, **Clouded Leopard** (3 colors each).
- Draft sprites: `sprites-draft.js` and `sprites-draft-batch2.js`, previews: `preview.png` and `preview-batch2.png`. Not in the game yet.

Status: built into `blip.pyw` (PROWL, PROWL_WEEK, event tiers), the web app and the Windows event screen. The event switches on by date (Dec 4 to 17).

## Update: 28 skins, a Blip after every 4

The track is now 7 groups of (4 skins at 5 points, then 1 new Blip at 10 points): 35 tiers, 210 points in all. The 28 skins are spread over the
7 existing cats (Lion, Tiger, Cheetah, Jaguar, Cat, Lynx, Snow Leopard), 4 each. All 7 new Blips are free for everyone once the event ends
(December 18). Skins are added after each cat's other colors (Classic included), so saved color numbers never move.

## Update 2: skins for everyday Blips

Each group is now: 1 big-cat skin (Lion, Tiger, Cheetah, Jaguar, Cat, Lynx, Snow Leopard), then 3 big-cat-colored skins for everyday Blips
(Blip, Bun, Dog, Fox, Bear, Wolf), then the new Blip. The new Prowl Week Blips don't get pass skins; they keep their own colors.

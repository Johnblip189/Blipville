# Pride Week (event plan, draft)

Follows Spiky Week (ends December 3), same rules and pause days.

- Pets that count: Lion, Tiger, Cheetah, Jaguar. Pet and feed them, plus daily quests, to earn event points.
- 4 event skins at **5 points each** (5, 10, 15, 20): Lion **Sunmane**, Tiger **Moonstripe**, Cheetah **Dawnspot**, Jaguar **Emberrose**.
- 3 new Blips at **10 points each** (30, 40, 50): **Lioness**, **Cougar**, **Panther** (3 colors each).
- Draft sprites: `sprites-draft.js`, preview: `preview.png`. Not in the game yet.

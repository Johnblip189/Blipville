✨ About Blipville Redux

Redux is the new Windows app: the same Blipville you know, with a brand-new way of drawing your pets. New in v0.5.1: 👻 ghosts! 10 of them drift by at night. Click one while it glows.

🚫 No more black flashes

The classic app hid a "magic pink" background, and Windows had to repaint it whenever you clicked a pet or opened a menu. Sometimes that showed black for a split second. Redux draws every pet as a real see-through picture and hands it straight to Windows, so nothing gets repainted and nothing flashes.

💾 Your town comes with you

Redux uses the same save as the classic app: same pets, names, hats, hearts, worlds and unlocks. Just quit the classic Blipville first, so the two don't both move your pets around.

🧩 Everything's still here

All 249 Blips and 790+ colors, Mystery Eggs, the hat shop, the Collection, cryptids, kaiju, World Legends, Worlds, weather, games of ball and tag, sound effects, Night Shift and the Beta Buddy gift.

🔄 Keeps itself up to date

When a new version is out, Redux asks, downloads it and restarts by itself. Right-click a pet → 🔄 Updates to check any time.

Getting it

  1. If the classic Blipville is running, right-click a pet → Goodbye everyone (quit).
  2. Download Blipville-Redux.exe and double-click it. No installer needed.
  3. Windows may say "protected your PC" the first time. Click More info → Run anyway.
  4. Your pets drop in from the top of the screen. Right-click one for everything else. 🎉

Found a bug in Redux? Right-click a pet → 💬 Suggestions & help → Get help / report a problem (and Copy error log helps a lot).

How to play
  • Tap a Blip to pet it 💖
  • Drag a Blip to pick it up. Let go to toss it!
  • Press and hold a Blip for its menu: feed, nap, rename, colors.
  • Caring earns hearts: pet +1 · feed +3 (+5 if hungry) · full nap +2 · daily treat +10
  • Spend 30 hearts on a 🥚 Mystery Egg to hatch one of the rare Blips.
  • 👣 Keep watching: every few minutes a cryptid sneaks through town. Tap the shadow with glowing eyes before it gets away to befriend it!
  • Brave enough? Tap 🌙 Night to play Night Shift. Survive until 6 AM to unlock the robo crew!
  • Buy hats & outfits from a Blip's menu. Tap 🔇 at the top to turn on sound effects.
  • Add Blipville to your home screen to play offline: Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android).
Blipville is free and open source. The desktop version lets your Blips walk around your computer screen: github.com/Johnblip189/blipville
